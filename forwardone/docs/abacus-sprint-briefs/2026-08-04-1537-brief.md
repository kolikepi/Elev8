Continue from the existing ForwardOne Sprint 2 project.

The objectives are:

1. Dynamic quotation workflows based on the selected transport service
2. Advanced multi-piece shipment handling
3. Carrier tariff import and automatic suggested cost calculation

====================================================

CORE PRINCIPLE

The quotation form must ask only for information that is genuinely required to calculate or review a transport quotation.

Do not force customers to enter full pickup, sender or operational booking details during the initial quotation stage when those details are not necessary.

Full operational details may be collected after the quotation is accepted and before the shipment is booked.

====================================================

SERVICE SELECTION

Before displaying the detailed quotation form, ask the customer to choose the required service category:

- Express Courier
- Road Freight / Groupage
- Air Freight
- Sea Freight

For Version 2.1:

- Express Courier must be fully implemented.
- Road Freight / Groupage must be fully implemented.
- Air Freight may use a basic quotation workflow.
- Sea Freight may remain a clearly marked placeholder.

Express Courier services include:

- FedEx Express
- FedEx Economy
- TNT Express
- TNT Economy
- DHL Express

Road Freight services include:

- Groupage
- LTL
- FTL
- Pallet Transport
- Dedicated Vehicle

The quote form must dynamically change according to the selected service category.

====================================================

EXPRESS COURIER QUOTATION WORKFLOW

For FedEx, TNT and DHL quotations, require only the minimum information needed to calculate or review a quotation.

Mandatory route fields:

- Import or Export
- Origin Country
- Destination Country
- Destination City
- Destination Postal Code, when applicable
- Preferred pickup date or ready date

For the origin side, Origin Country must be mandatory.

The following origin details must be optional during quotation:

- Sender name
- Sender company
- Sender full address
- Sender postal code
- Sender phone
- Sender email
- Pickup contact
- Exact pickup address

Do not require full sender details merely to prepare an express quotation.

Mandatory destination or receiver information:

- Receiver name or company
- Destination country
- Destination city
- Destination postal code, when applicable

Receiver phone, email and full street address may remain optional during quotation and become mandatory after the quotation is accepted, before booking or AWB creation.

Mandatory shipment fields:

- Shipment type
- Commercial or Personal shipment
- Number of pieces
- Individual piece weights
- Individual piece dimensions
- Goods description
- Commercial value for commercial shipments
- Currency
- Dangerous Goods: Yes or No

Conditional mandatory fields:

- UN Number when Dangerous Goods is Yes
- MSDS upload when required
- Commercial Invoice when required
- Packing List when required
- Incoterm for commercial shipments when relevant

The Express quotation workflow should be short, fast and easy to complete.

====================================================

PIECE CALCULATIONS

Calculate independently for every individual piece:

- Actual weight
- Volumetric weight
- Chargeable weight
- Cubic volume

Use:

Chargeable weight per piece =
the greater of actual weight or volumetric weight

When a row has multiple identical pieces, apply the calculation to each piece before multiplying by quantity.

Total shipment values:

- Total number of pieces
- Total actual weight
- Total volumetric weight
- Total chargeable weight
- Total cubic volume

The volumetric divisor must not be hardcoded globally.

Allow the administrator to configure a volumetric divisor by:

- Carrier
- Service
- Import or Export
- Origin country
- Destination country
- Package type
- Effective date
- Weight or dimension unit

Examples may include divisors such as 5000, 4000 or other carrier-specific values, but all divisors must be configurable by the administrator.

Show a clear calculation breakdown for every piece and for the complete shipment.
