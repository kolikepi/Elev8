CORE OBJECTIVE

ForwardOne must support operational changes AFTER a shipment is booked.

Quotation revision is used BEFORE booking.

After booking, use booking/shipment amendments instead of editing historical quotation or shipment data directly.

Historical operational data must NEVER be silently overwritten.

ForwardOne must distinguish between:

1. QUOTED DATA
2. BOOKED DATA
3. ACTUAL CARRIER DATA

Example:

Quoted weight: 40 kg

Booked weight: 43 kg

Actual carrier weight: 47 kg

All three must remain available and auditable.
